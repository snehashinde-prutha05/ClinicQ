About ClinicQ

Waiting at a small clinic with no idea of your turn is stressful. ClinicQ gives every patient a token, shows their place in the queue and an estimated time, and applies the same fair rules to walk-ins and online bookings.

Why small clinics need it Three everyday problems

Nobody knows their turn

Patients sit for hours, or step out and miss their turn.

Who goes first?

Without clear rules, online and walk-in patients end up arguing.

A busy front desk

Walk-ins, emergencies and no-shows are juggled from memory.

How it works Three steps for a patient

  1. 1. Get a token

    Book online in about ten seconds, or ask at the desk. You get a token number and a time to reach the clinic.

  2. 2. Reach the clinic

    Tap I'm here on your token page, or tell reception. You join the queue in token order.

  3. 3. Watch your turn

    See your place, the estimated wait and the time you will be called. The page updates by itself.

Fair by design The rules everyone follows

One line for everyone

Online and walk-in patients share one queue and one number sequence.

Emergencies first

An emergency is seen next, without interrupting the patient already with the doctor.

A little grace if you are late

Arrive up to 15 minutes after your time and keep your place. Later than that, you join behind those already waiting.

A second chance

If you are called and not there within 5 minutes, your turn is skipped. You can rejoin once.

Who uses ClinicQ Each person sees only what they need

Patients

Get a token, follow the queue and cancel if plans change. No account needed.

Receptionists

Issue walk-in slips, check patients in, flag emergencies and handle no-shows.

Doctors

Call the next patient, start and finish consultations, and see the day's load.

Your privacy

Only your name, phone number and token are stored. There are no medical records. Your token page shows just your first name and a masked phone number.

Quick answers

Do I need an account?

No. Enter your name and phone number to get a token. Only clinic staff sign in.

I don't have a smartphone. Can I still use it?

Yes. Ask the receptionist for a walk-in token. You get a printed slip with your token number and time.

What does the estimated time mean?

It is the number of patients ahead of you multiplied by the average consultation time today. It is an estimate, so it can change.

I lost my token page. What now?

Open Track my token and enter your phone number and token number.
